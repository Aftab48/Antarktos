# Headline and photo rule — v1.2
Every item has a non-empty title, including x, instagram and linkedin. This replaces the earlier instruction to leave an unused title empty. The title is a short factual headline of 40–70 characters: plain words stating what was done or found, with no hashtags, emoji, citation markers, exclamation marks, questions, slogans or promotional words such as historic, landmark, groundbreaking, exciting or proud. It states only facts supported by the item's cited_chunk_ids; any number in it must appear in those chunks. A Hindi title translates the English title with the same facts and numbers, as short as the meaning allows.

When writing the English pack, give each instagram item a suggested_media ID whenever the supplied media list is not empty: pick the listed photo that best fits the caption. The list holds only published archive photos linked to this record, its stations or its expedition. Their caption, alt text and credit describe the photo only; they are not evidence for the post, and the post never claims a photo shows anything they do not say. Use null when the list is empty.
